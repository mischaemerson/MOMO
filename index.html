What MOMO does

Built for questions
that take years to answer.

01
🔬

Literature
Synthesis

Point MOMO at a disease, a drug, or a mechanism. It reads across PubMed, Cochrane, and preprint servers and returns a structured summary of what the field actually agrees on — and where it doesn't.

02
⚠️

Contradiction
Detection

Medical literature contradicts itself constantly. MOMO flags conflicting findings across studies, weights them by sample size, recency, and journal quality, and tells you which side of a debate is stronger.

03
🧬

Genomic
Cross-reference

MOMO connects genetic markers to published clinical outcomes. Ask about a mutation, get back every relevant trial, pathway, and known drug interaction — mapped and ranked by evidence strength.

04
📋

Systematic
Review Assistant

Systematic reviews take months. MOMO handles the screening, data extraction, and bias assessment in hours — giving your team a head start that used to require a full research coordinator.

05
💊

Drug Interaction
Mapping

Before you design a combination therapy, MOMO surfaces every known interaction, adverse event report, and mechanistic conflict across FDA filings, trial data, and case literature.

06
📡

Preprint
Monitoring

The field moves before journals do. MOMO watches bioRxiv and medRxiv daily, alerts you when new findings touch your research area, and flags anything that challenges your working hypotheses.

The process

How
MOMO
reads the field

01
You define the scope

Set your disease area, drug class, or research question. MOMO filters its index to the studies that actually matter for your work — not everything ever published.

02
MOMO reads across sources simultaneously

It pulls from PubMed, Cochrane, ClinicalTrials.gov, FDA filings, and preprint servers at the same time — cross-referencing findings as it reads rather than after.

03
Contradictions get surfaced, not buried

Most tools summarize what studies say. MOMO specifically looks for where studies disagree, why they might disagree, and how much the disagreement should concern you.

04
You get findings, not a reading list

Every response cites its sources with direct links. You always know what MOMO is confident about, what it's inferring, and what the field hasn't settled yet.
